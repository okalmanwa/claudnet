Frequently asked questions

How much does CCTV installation cost in Kisii?

The price depends on how many cameras you need, the type of cameras, how many days of recording you want to keep and how far the cables need to run. Send us a WhatsApp message on 0792 271 569 describing your property and we will give you a clear quote before any work starts.

Can I watch my CCTV cameras on my phone?

Yes. We set up the viewing app on your phone so you can watch live and replay recordings. Remote viewing needs internet at the property, and CLAUDNET internet starts at Ksh 1,000 a month.

Do the cameras work at night?

Yes. We install cameras with night vision so your compound, shop or office is still covered after dark.

How long is CCTV footage stored?

That depends on the size of the hard drive and the number of cameras. Tell us how many days of footage you want to keep and we will size the recorder to match.

Do you install CCTV for businesses, schools and churches?

Yes. We install CCTV for homes, shops, supermarkets, schools, hospitals, churches and offices across Kisii County.

Do you install CCTV outside Kisii Town?

Yes. We install across Kisii County, including Suneka, Keroka, Ogembo, Nyamache, Marani and Tabaka, and in Homa Bay County too.
